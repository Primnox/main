# Primnox Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thanks for wanting to contribute to Primnox. Before your first contribution is
merged, we ask you to agree to this short CLA.

**Why this exists (plain English):** it lets Primnox stay free and open for
individuals *and* sustainable for its maintainer. It does **not** take your
rights away — you keep full ownership of your code and can reuse it anywhere.
It just gives the maintainer the permissions needed to ship the project,
defend it, and offer paid commercial licenses to companies that want to use
Primnox commercially. This is the same model used by GitLab, Grafana, Sentry,
and many other projects.

## How to sign

Add this line to the description of your **first** pull request (one time only):

> I have read and agree to the Primnox CLA.
> Signed: `<your full name>` `<your email>`

That's it — once it's on record we can merge your work.
