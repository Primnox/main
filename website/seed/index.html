What makes a phrase valid.

A BIP-39 mnemonic is not twelve random words. It is 128 bits of entropy plus a four-bit SHA-256 checksum, packed into 132 bits and sliced into twelve 11-bit indices into a fixed 2048-word list. The last word is partly derived, not chosen.

That checksum is why a mistyped word gets caught at restore time instead of silently producing the wrong key. The badge above reflects a real verification: the phrase is decoded back to entropy and its checksum re-derived before it is shown to you.
