The
Thesis.

You can't trust a server. You can trust local code.

Every assistant worth using needs context, and every assistant that has your context can leak it. The industry's answer has been a promise in a privacy policy. That isn't an answer, it's a liability transfer.

Primnox's answer is architectural: keep the raw context on your machine, scrub it on-device, and send only de-identified text to the frontier model. You get the good brain. It never learns who you are. As of v0.1.1 this is shipping, not planned.
