01 / Product

Six Things
It Actually Does.

Primnox is one application, not a suite. Everything below shares the same data model, the same command bar, and the same memory.

01
AI with Personality
Not a yes-bot. Primnox pushes back when it matters, roasts you when you're being dramatic, and helps you build a life rather than tick boxes. It remembers quietly — no "I've saved that to memory" theatre — and it will never guilt you about an unfinished task.
↗
02
Unified Personal OS
Calendar, tasks, notes and AI share one underlying object. A task is a calendar block. A note is an event. Schedule in natural language from anywhere, and press Ctrl+K to command all of it from a single palette.
↗
03
Knowledge Graph
Primnox maps the people, topics and ideas you mention, automatically. Nodes size by connectedness, colour by type, and highlight their neighbours on hover. Everything you've told it stays searchable; nothing gets lost in a chat scrollback.
↗
04
BIP-39 Encrypted Backups
Your seed phrase, your data. Back up to S3, B2, R2, Google Drive, Dropbox or your own box, auto-syncing every 24 hours. The encryption key derives from a phrase only you hold, so we cannot decrypt your backup even if we wanted to.
↗
05
Smart Paste
Copy anything, get AI-enhanced context on a global shortcut. Rewrite an email, fix a stack trace, translate, summarise. It appears when called and stays out of the way otherwise.
↗
06
Local Privacy Scrubbing Live · v0.1.1
Privacy Mirror swaps names, emails, passwords and keys for placeholders before your context reaches a cloud AI, then reverses the substitution in the reply so you still read real names. Deterministic regex rules backstop the model. It only engages for cloud calls — local models get the raw text. How it works →
